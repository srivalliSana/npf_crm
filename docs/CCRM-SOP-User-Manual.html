What this is. CCRM is the admissions CRM used to capture, assign, and progress student leads through to enrolment — leads come in from Meta/Facebook & Instagram ads, your own website's inquiry form, bulk Excel/CSV uploads, and partner integrations (e.g. CU EDU's registration form), and are worked by counsellors through to payment and admission.

Part 1 is process — what to do, in what order, and who's responsible. Part 2 is reference — what each screen does and how to use it. Every organisation on the platform (Centurion, CU EDU, and any future tenant) uses the same CCRM instance, each with its own login URL, branding, and fully separate data.

Part 1

Standard Operating Procedures

1.1 Roles & Access Levels

Within Admin/Manager, an Entity Access grant (e.g. CUTM, CUTMAP, FTL) further restricts which lead sets a Counselor sees, set per-user in User Management.

1.2 Lead Lifecycle Policy

Every lead moves through a fixed set of stages. Stage changes drive dashboard counts, reports, and — at one specific point — automatically create an Application.

Untouched→ Contacted→ Follow Up→ Interested→ Campus Visit→ Process for Payment→ Payment Success
Not Interested Invalid Number No Response — off-ramps, reachable from most stages

Rule: the moment a lead reaches Process for Payment, the system automatically creates its matching Application record — counsellors never create that Application by hand.

Rule: a lead marked Not Interested must have a reason attached (Budget, Already Enrolled, Not Reachable, etc.) — this is required, not optional, and feeds the Reports page.

Rule: Unable to Connect is not a dead end — it moves the lead to Follow Up so it re-enters the working queue, rather than silently disappearing.

1.3 Daily Counsellor Workflow

  1. Open Dashboard first — check your Untouched and Follow Up counts for the day.
  2. Work Lead Manager top-down within your assigned list: call, log the outcome (Call Log icon), and move the stage immediately after the call — don't batch stage updates for later.
  3. Every call gets logged via the phone icon on the lead row, even a "no answer" — outcome tracking depends on this, and Call Report/Productivity dashboards read directly from it.
  4. For an interested lead, open the lead and fill in Lead Details (parent info, 10th/12th marks, address) as it comes up in conversation — this data auto-fills the Provisional Admission Letter later, so it's much easier to capture it now than to chase it after payment.
  5. Check Tasks and Calendar for anything scheduled (interviews, follow-up calls) before logging off.
  6. End of day: clear your Not Interested queue with reasons recorded — don't leave stage changes half-done.

1.4 Assignment & Transfer Policy

New leads auto-assign round-robin across active counsellors granted the relevant entity (CUTM/CUTMAP/FTL/etc.) — nobody hand-picks who gets the next lead.

Transfers: Admins/Managers can move a lead to another counsellor instantly. A Counsellor requesting a transfer instead creates a pending request that an Admin/Manager must approve or reject on the Transfer Approvals page — a counsellor can never move a lead to themselves or a peer unilaterally.

Bulk reassignment: use Lead Manager's multi-select + "Assign to…" for reassigning many leads at once (e.g. when a counsellor leaves).

1.5 Application & Payment Processing SOP

  1. Application auto-creates when the lead hits Process for Payment (see 1.2).
  2. Counsellor fills Admission Details on the student's profile before generating a payment link — this populates the Provisional Admission Letter and is required, not optional.
  3. Generate the payment link: choose Online (Razorpay) or Offline (bank/cash). Online auto-fetches the UTR on success; Offline requires the counsellor or student to type in the UTR/reference number manually.
  4. Submitting a UTR sets the application to Payment Done — this is not final. It sits in a queue for Accounts.
  5. Finance/Accounts reviews and clicks Approve on the Payments page, moving it to Paid — only then does the Provisional Admission Letter auto-send by email.
Never mark a payment Approved without a verified UTR/transaction reference — Approval is the point of no return for the auto-sent admission letter.

1.6 Document Verification Procedure

Required documents: 10th Marksheet, 12th Marksheet, Aadhar Card, Passport Photo, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Caste Certificate, Income Certificate, Character Certificate, Medical Certificate.

  1. Student/counsellor uploads via the Documents tab on the student's file (PDF/JPG/PNG, under 5MB).
  2. Every upload starts at status Pending.
  3. Admin/Manager reviews and marks Verified or Rejected — a rejected document should be followed by a note/call asking for a re-upload.
  4. A share link can be generated and sent to the student so they can upload directly without a CRM login.

1.7 User & Role Management

Creating a user: Admin → User Management → Add User. Set role, team, entity access, and a temporary password — the new user should change it on first login.

Deactivating, not deleting: when someone leaves, set their status to Inactive rather than deleting the account — this preserves their historical lead/call/payment attribution in reports.

Password resets generate a new temporary password and email it automatically — Admins should never share a password over chat/SMS.

Excluded from auto-assign: toggle this for anyone temporarily off rotation (leave, training) so round-robin skips them without changing their role.

1.8 Tenant Onboarding (Platform Admin only)

  1. Platform Admin → Tenants → Add Tenant. Set organisation name, URL slug (e.g. cuedu → crm.cutmap.ac.in/cuedu/login), and the first admin's email.
  2. One-time credentials are shown once — copy and hand them to the organisation's admin immediately, they aren't recoverable from the UI afterward (only resettable).
  3. Set allowed login domains (e.g. cutm.ac.in) so Google Sign-In only accepts that organisation's real staff emails.
  4. Optionally set a Lead ID prefix (e.g. CUEDU26) if this tenant has an external integration quoting lead reference IDs — otherwise it defaults to CULDAI26/CULDSM26.
  5. Use View Leads to check in on a tenant as its own admin when helping them troubleshoot — this is logged.
Every email address is unique per organisation, not globally — the same person can have a completely separate account in two different tenants.

1.9 Duplicate & Data Integrity Policy

A lead is a duplicate within its organisation if it shares a mobile number or email with an existing lead — checked automatically on every create path (manual add, bulk upload, Meta webhook, public inquiry API).

Bulk upload lets you choose how duplicates are handled: Skip (default, leaves existing leads alone), Update existing (refreshes name/course/source on the match), or Import all (allows intentional duplicates — rarely correct).

Never manually delete a lead to "fix" a duplicate — use Skip/Update handling, or merge context via a Note instead, so the original creation timestamp and history survive.

1.10 Integration & Credential Management Policy

All third-party credentials (Meta Page Access Token, SMTP, Razorpay, etc.) are entered once on the Integrations page and encrypted at rest — the CRM never displays a saved secret by default, only a masked placeholder.

Click the eye icon next to a saved field to decrypt and view it on demand (Admin only, and logged) — only do this when you actually need to copy/verify the value, not as a matter of routine.

When a partner integration (e.g. CU EDU) needs an API key, generate one specifically for that purpose rather than sharing an internal credential — ask your platform admin.

Never paste a live API key or access token into chat, email, or a shared document unencrypted — including this manual. Reference "see Integrations page" instead.
Part 2

User Manual

2.1 Getting Started

Each organisation on CCRM has its own login address and its own branding — you'll only ever see your own organisation's data.

Centurion staff
crm.cutmap.ac.in/login
Other organisations
crm.cutmap.ac.in/<your-org-slug>/login
Sign in
Email + password, or Google Sign-In if your work email's domain is on the organisation's allow-list
Forgot password
"Forgot password?" on the login screen sends a one-time code to your email

2.2 Dashboard

Your homepage after login. What you see depends on your role.

Counsellor
Your own lead counts by stage only — Total, Untouched, Interested, Follow Up, Not Interested.

Admin / Manager
Organisation-wide KPI tiles, the CUTM/CUTMAP split, Leads by Source, and the Stage Summary matrix (every counsellor × every stage, exportable to CSV).

2.3 Lead Manager

The main working table — search, filter, and act on leads without opening each one.

Filtering

Domain tabs (All / CUTM / CUTMAP) at the top, plus Stage, Owner, Source, State, and date-range filters. Search matches name, email, or mobile.

Per-row actions

WhatsApp, RCS message, log a call, email, schedule a follow-up, transfer, or delete (Counsellors can only delete their own Untouched leads — Admins can delete any).

Bulk actions (select multiple rows)

WhatsApp/RCS/Email campaigns, Drip sequence enrolment, bulk reassignment, bulk delete (Admin/Manager only).

Add Lead / Bulk Upload

Add one lead manually (checks for duplicates as you type the mobile number), or import a CSV/Excel file — the importer auto-detects columns and shows a preview before committing.

2.4 Student File (Lead / Application Detail)

Click any lead's name to open their full file — profile, stage journey, and a tabbed workspace.

  • Lead Journey — click any stage bubble to move the lead directly, no dropdown needed.
  • Lead Details tab — student, parent/guardian, address, 10th/12th academic history, joining programme. Click Edit Information to fill it in.
  • Timeline — automatic log of every stage change, note, call, and email on this lead.
  • Notes, Tickets, Calendar Pro — free-text notes, support tickets, and interview/call scheduling scoped to this student.
  • Documents tab — upload and check verification status without leaving the file.
  • Admission Details / Payment buttons appear automatically once the student has an Application.

2.5 Applications

Application Manager lists every application across your organisation (or your own, as a Counsellor) with Payment Status, Form Status, and quick actions.

Application Stage runs Unverified → Verified → Application Started → Payment Approved → Application Submitted → Enrolment. Generate a payment link straight from the row, or open the application for full detail.

2.6 Tasks & Calendar

General to-dos (Tasks) and scheduled events/interviews (Calendar). Both can be tied to a specific lead from that lead's own file, or created standalone for anything else on your plate.

2.7 Payments

The full payment ledger — every transaction against every application, with its status.

Payment Pending→ Payment Done(Accounts approves)→ Paid

Finance/Admin use the Approve action here to move Payment Done → Paid, which triggers the Provisional Admission Letter email automatically.

2.8 Documents

Organisation-wide view of every uploaded document across all students, filterable by status — the same Verify/Reject actions available on an individual student's file, but for bulk review.

2.9 Campaigns & Communication

Email Campaigns — compose and send to a filtered lead list; delivery is tracked per recipient.

Drip Workflows — enrol leads into a multi-step, timed sequence of automated touches.

Social Comments — Facebook/Instagram comments on your ads are auto-captured here and turned into leads automatically.

Comms Report — a rollup of everything sent (WhatsApp, RCS, Email, SMS) across the organisation.

2.10 Reports & Analytics

Reports, Analytics, Productivity, Call Activity Report, and Leaderboard cover conversion funnels, per-counsellor performance, call outcomes, and team rankings — all exportable, all scoped to your role's visibility (a Counsellor only ever sees their own numbers reflected in these).

2.11 User Management Admin

Add/edit users, assign roles and entity access, reset passwords, promote/revoke Super Admin, and manage custom Teams & Roles beyond the four system defaults. See 1.7 for the procedure.

2.12 Integrations Admin

Connect Meta Lead Ads, Google Sign-In, SMTP email, Razorpay, RCS messaging, and more. Each card shows a Connected badge once configured, a Test Connection button, and an eye icon to reveal a saved secret on demand. See 1.10 for policy.

2.13 Platform Admin — Tenants Platform Admin

Create organisations, edit their name/plan/allowed domains/lead-ID prefix, manage their admin accounts (add new, promote an existing user, reset credentials), suspend/reactivate, and use View Leads to step into a tenant as its own admin when helping them troubleshoot. See 1.8.

2.14 Settings & Profile

Update your own name, photo, and mobile number (used for WhatsApp lead-assignment alerts and click-to-call) under Profile. Organisation-wide branding, entities, and lead-stage customisation live under Organization (Admin only).

2.15 Troubleshooting & Support

"No leads found" as a Counsellor — you only see leads assigned to you; ask an Admin to check your entity access and assignment.

Can't log into another organisation's URL — each organisation's URL only accepts its own accounts (plus Platform Admins); the same email can have a separate account per organisation.

Payment link not generating — check Razorpay credentials under Integrations are current, or use the Offline/UTR path instead.

Still stuck? Use the Ask CU AI assistant in the top bar, or contact your organisation's Admin.
