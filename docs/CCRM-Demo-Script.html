CCRM · live demo runbook

Demo script — start to finish, ~18 minutes

A walk-through in one continuous story: a student's whole journey from first inquiry to ERP access, followed by the reporting/admin layer. Each step says exactly what to click and what to say. Read it once beforehand so the clicks feel natural — you shouldn't need to look at this screen while actually presenting.

⏱️ ~18 min total, trim Section 6–7 if short on time
🎭 2 roles — Admin tab + a private/incognito tab for student-facing parts
📧 1 test inbox you control, for OTP/links

Before you start

Open two browser windows. Window A: logged into the CRM as Admin — this is what you present from 90% of the time. Window B: a private/incognito window, logged out — this is "the student's" view (public inquiry form, OTP page, document upload).

Pick one real test lead now and use the same person throughout the whole demo — one continuous story lands better than jumping between random records. Use an email address you can actually check live (for the OTP/document-link steps).

Have a second tab open on your own inbox, ready to alt-tab to when an email needs to visibly arrive (admission letter, OTP, document link, ERP access).

1 · Open — the story you're telling

Set up the arc before you touch anything30 sec

  1. Don't open the CRM yet — say the sentence first.
    Say: "I'm going to show you one student going from their first click on our website, all the way through to getting access to ERP — live, in this system, right now. Then I'll show what the team sees on the other side: dashboards, reports, and an AI assistant that answers questions from real data."

2 · Lead capture

The public inquiry form Live2 min

  1. Window B (incognito) → go to the public inquiry page (/apply).
    Say: "This is what a student sees when they click 'Apply' from an ad or the website."
  2. Fill in your test lead's real name, email, mobile, and course. Submit.
    Say: "No login needed — this is the exact form real applicants use."
  3. Switch to Window A (Admin) → Lead Manager. Refresh.
    Say: "And there it is — already in the CRM, already duplicate-checked, already round-robin assigned to a counsellor." Point at the Owner column.

3 · Counselling

Working the lead Live2 min

  1. Click into the lead → open the Student File.
    Say: "Everything about this student lives in one file — profile, timeline, calls, notes."
  2. Click the Lead Journey bubble to move the stage forward (e.g. Contacted → Interested).
    Say: "A counsellor updates the stage as they work the lead — no dropdown hunting, just click where they are in the journey." Mention the Timeline auto-logging the change.
  3. Point at (don't necessarily click) WhatsApp / Call / Email icons.
    Say: "Every touch — call, WhatsApp, email — is logged automatically against this student, which is what feeds the reports later."

4 · Application & payment

From interested to paid Live3 min

  1. Move the lead's stage to Process for Payment.
    Say: "The moment they hit this stage, the system automatically creates their Application — nobody types that in by hand." Switch to Applications to show the new row with an auto-generated application number.
  2. Open the application → Generate Payment Link (Offline mode is fastest for a live demo).
    Say: "Counsellor generates a payment link — online via Razorpay, or offline if the student's already paid by bank transfer." Enter a fake UTR and submit.
  3. Go to Payments → find the row → click Approve (Finance/Admin action).
    Say: "Finance approves it — and watch, two things fire automatically." Alt-tab to your inbox.
  4. Show the inbox: Provisional Admission Letter (PDF) and a second email with a 6-digit OTP.
    Say: "The admission letter goes out immediately — and this OTP email is the start of the next phase, which most CRMs don't have at all."

5 · Post-admission — the new build

Email verification → documents → semester fee → ERP Live4 min

This whole section is what was built this cycle — it's worth slowing down here, this is the newest and most differentiated part of the demo.

  1. Window B → go to /verify-email, enter the admission number + OTP from the inbox.
    Say: "Before anything else, we confirm this is really the student's email — not just a name someone typed in a form."
  2. Show the success screen, then alt-tab to the inbox — a second email just arrived with a unique document-upload link.
    Say: "Verified email unlocks a one-time secure upload link — sent automatically, no counsellor had to chase it."
  3. Click that link (still Window B) → upload one test file against any document type.
    Say: "Student uploads straight from their phone or laptop — no login required — and it lands in the counsellor's Documents tab as Pending."
  4. Window A → Student File → Documents tab → mark the upload Verified.
    Say: "Counsellor verifies it like any other document."
  5. Same file → click Unlock Semester Fee → then Generate Semester Fee Link.
    Say: "This is deliberately a manual gate — an Admin decides when a student's ready for 1st-semester billing, it never happens by accident." Point out this is a completely separate payment from the application fee — check Payments, now shows two rows for this student.
  6. Approve that semester payment the same way as step 4.3.
    Say: "Same approval flow as before — and now watch the final piece." Alt-tab to inbox.
  7. Show the ERP-access email, then Window A → the Student File now shows an "🎓 ERP Access Granted" badge.
    Say: "Student's told to use their admission number as a login ID for now — and the CRM tracks that this student has crossed from 'applicant' to 'active student.' That handoff point is exactly where the roadmap picks up toward a real LMS/ERP connection."

6 · The team's side

Dashboards, reports, and Ask CU AI Live4 min

  1. Dashboard (role-scoped landing page).
    Say: "A counsellor only ever sees their own numbers here — an Admin sees the whole funnel." Point at KPI tiles and the stage-summary matrix.
  2. Website Leads Dashboard (sidebar → Website Leads → Overview).
    Say: "This tracks our other inbound channels — FTL, GTIB, GTTECH, ESSE — each with total, not-contacted, and assignment numbers, live off the database."
  3. Open Ask CU AI (top bar) → type: "How many total leads do we have?" then a second question, e.g. "How many applications have payment pending?"
    Say: "This isn't a chatbot with canned answers — it's genuinely querying the live database every time. Ask it something right now if you want." (Live audience Q&A here is a strong moment — the numbers really do change if someone adds a lead mid-demo.)
  4. Productivity Report (Admin/Manager only).
    Say: "Per-counsellor breakdown — leads, follow-ups, applications, conversions — the numbers management actually reviews weekly."

7 · Admin & platform (optional, time permitting)

User management, integrations, multi-tenant 2 min

  1. User Management → show role assignment (Admin/Manager/Counsellor/Finance) and entity access.
    Say: "Every permission — who sees which leads, who can approve payments — is controlled here, no code changes needed."
  2. Integrations page → scroll past the connected cards (Meta, SMTP, WhatsApp, Razorpay, EasyGoIVR).
    Say: "All of this — ads, email, WhatsApp, payments, calling — is wired directly into the CRM, not bolted on separately."

8 · Full feature tour — everything else in the sidebar

One line each — walk the sidebar top to bottom5–8 min

Sections 2–6 already covered Dashboard, Leads, Website Leads, Applications, Payments, Documents, Analytics, Productivity, and Ask CU AI in the story. This is everything not yet touched — go down the sidebar in order, one sentence per item, click in only where marked.

Day-to-day work

Tasks click in
General to-dos and follow-up reminders — standalone or tied to a specific lead.
Calendar
Scheduled interviews and follow-up calls in a day/week view.
Transfers
Where Admin/Manager approves or rejects a counsellor's request to hand off a lead — nobody reassigns a lead to themselves unilaterally.

Marketing & outreach

Campaigns click in
Every ad campaign's budget, spend, leads, and conversions in one place.
Email Camps
Compose and send to a filtered lead list, with delivery tracked per recipient.
Drip Flows
Multi-step, timed WhatsApp/SMS/Email sequences — e.g. an automatic payment-reminder cadence.
Social Comments
Facebook/Instagram comments on ads are auto-captured here and turned into leads automatically.

Reporting

Reports click in
Conversion funnels, source-to-enrollment tracking, publisher benchmarking.
Call Report
Call outcomes and connect rates, per counsellor.
Comms
Every WhatsApp/SMS/Email/Call sent across the whole organisation, rolled into one view.
Leaderboard
Team rankings by conversion — the one page counsellors actually check unprompted.
Upload Logs
History of every bulk lead import, with duplicate-handling results.

Admin & platform

Users click in
Create accounts, assign roles/entity access, reset passwords — covered briefly in Section 7.
Integrations
Meta, SMTP, WhatsApp, Razorpay, EasyGoIVR, Google Sheets, AWS S3 backup — covered in Section 7.
Server Health
Live system status — uptime, DB connectivity — for the technically curious in the room.
Security & Access
Login activity and access-control overview.
Organization
Branding, entity structure (CUTM/CUTMAP/GT entities), and lead-stage customization — this is what lets the same CRM serve a completely different organisation with different branding and a different pipeline, with zero code changes.
Settings / Help
Personal profile, notification preferences, and documentation — skip unless asked.

Pacing tip: Sections 2–6 are the "show, don't tell" part — slow down, let the emails actually arrive, let the AI actually think. Section 8 is the "yes, we thought of that too" part — fast, one sentence per item, click in only on the three or four marked pages. Don't let Section 8 drag; it's breadth, not depth.

Close

Say: "Everything you just watched — lead, counselling, application, documents, both payments, ERP handoff — was one continuous, real flow, not a mockup. What's next on the roadmap is extending this same pattern past admission: academic services, exams, semester re-registration, placement, and alumni — the same 'one student, one file, automatic handoffs' idea, just carried further down the lifecycle."

If something doesn't cooperate live

Email doesn't arrive in time
Keep talking — narrate what should have happened, then check the inbox again after the next step. Never sit in silence refreshing.
A number looks "wrong" or surprising on a dashboard
Say "that's live data, let's see what's actually behind it" and click in — real numbers are more convincing than round demo numbers.
Ask CU AI is slow to respond
Narrate: "it's actually running a live database query right now, not playing back a script" — the wait itself supports the pitch.
Running short on time
Cut Section 7 entirely and shorten Section 6 to just the Ask CU AI live-question moment — that's the highest-impact single beat in the whole demo.
