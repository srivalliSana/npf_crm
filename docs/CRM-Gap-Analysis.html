Headline finding. Everything from Lead Generation through Admission (modules 1–5) is real and working — this is a genuine, demoable end-to-end flow. Everything after Admission — Onboarding, Academic Services, Exams, Re-registration, Completion, Placement, Alumni (modules 6–12) — does not exist in any form. Automation (13) is limited to campaign drip sequences, not the cross-module event triggers the spec describes. There is no LMS integration, no marketing website beyond one landing page, and no analytics/attribution layer.

§3What this means for Friday

The honest story to tell: "Phase 1 — Lead to Admission — is live and working end-to-end today." That's steps 1–11, and it's a real, running system, not a mockup. Demo it live: a lead comes in from the landing page, gets assigned, gets counselled, converts to an application, gets documents verified, pays, and gets an admission letter auto-emailed.

Steps 12–17 have zero underlying implementation — there's no realistic way to build working Onboarding, Academic Services, Exams, Re-registration, Completion, Placement, or Alumni features by Friday. Trying to fake these with static screens risks the demo reading as thin the moment anyone asks a follow-up question.

Recommended framing: show the live Lead→Admission flow as "Phase 1, shipped," then present modules 6–17 as a named roadmap (Phase 2: Onboarding + Academic Services; Phase 3: Exams + Re-registration + Completion; Phase 4: Placement + Alumni) with the automation engine (module 13) called out as the connective layer that makes the whole thing feel like one system rather than a database — this directly answers the "digital ecosystem, not a database" line in the email without overclaiming what exists today.
